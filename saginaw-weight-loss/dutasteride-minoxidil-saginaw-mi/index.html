    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Saginaw

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Blocking both enzymes cuts DHT further than finasteride can. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil adds a separate benefit by lengthening the growth phase. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For
