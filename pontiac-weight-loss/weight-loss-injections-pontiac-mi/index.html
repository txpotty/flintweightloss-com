    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Pontiac

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. They copy a hormone your gut releases after eating, which slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps regulate blood sugar after a meal. Semaglutide targets one receptor. Tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which may explain its larger average effect in trials.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. A BMI of 27 or above counts too when paired with a condition tied to weight, like type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea or high cholesterol. They work alongside diet and exercise. They are not for pregnancy or certain thyroid cancer histories.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Pontiac

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy starts at 0.25 mg and moves up every four weeks to 0.5, 1, 1.7 and finally 2.4 mg, with some patients holding at 1.7 mg. Zepbound begins at 2.5 mg and can go up by 2.5 mg at a time to a top of 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. The first month tends to show small changes on the scale since the dose is low, while stomach upset clusters right after each shot. Large trials reported average losses near 15 percent with semaglutide 2.4 mg over 68 weeks and near 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Pontiac

    Nausea is the classic one. Others include constipation, diarrhea, heartburn and a small red bump where the needle went in, mostly early and around dose increases. Smaller meals, protein first and plenty of water help most people. Rarer serious risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and dehydration-related kidney strain.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Your provider then reviews every medication you take, looking hardest at insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills, since tirzepatide can make oral contraceptives less reliable after dose changes. Medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in your family rules out GLP-1 injections. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Pontiac

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro contain the same active ingredients but are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes, not weight loss alone. Compounded versions of semaglutide and tirzepatide come from licensed compounding pharmacies and lack FDA approval, which means no FDA review of their safety, effectiveness or manufacturing. Some patients choose them for availability.
